Multi Timer for Android

Each timer runs on its own. Start, pause, reset, change, or delete any of them without touching the others. The sample timers on the left are live, and the 10-second chip lets you see what happens when one finishes.

Tap anywhere first if you want to hear the alarm sound; browsers block audio until you interact.

New in this version

  • −1 minSits next to +1 min. Disabled once a minute or less is left, so it never ends a timer by accident.
  • EditThe pencil opens the timer in the same panel as New timer. Changing the label or color keeps it running as is. Changing the duration of a running or paused timer asks for confirmation, then starts it again from the new time.
  • App soundsFive original 8-bit sounds made for Multi Timer: Power chime (the default), Pixel bell, Beeper, Gentle rise and Jingle. Built like old console sound: pulse and triangle waves, stepped volume, fast arpeggios and 8-bit depth. They are the real files the app would embed, about 90 KB in total. The phone's own alarm sounds stay available below them.
  • SoundsSettings (gear icon) has a default alarm sound. Each timer can override it from the Sound row when you create or edit it, and shows its own sound in the status line. Tap a sound in the list to hear it.
  • SettingsThe gear icon opens Settings: Theme (System default, Light, Dark) and the default alarm sound.
  • AccessibilityEvery text and control meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes: text at least 4.5:1, control outlines and timer colors at least 3:1.
  • DeleteAsks for confirmation first. As a safety net, a snackbar offers Undo for a few seconds after deleting.

How the app works

  • BackgroundTimers keep counting with the app closed. Each one stores its end time, so nothing drifts.
  • AlarmsAlarmManager.setExactAndAllowWhileIdle fires even in Doze. A finished timer rings with the alarm sound until you stop it.
  • NotificationsOne notification per timer with a live countdown and Pause, +1 min and Reset buttons. Asks for POST_NOTIFICATIONS on first launch (required since Android 13).
  • ThemesFollows the phone's light or dark setting. Light uses a white background with near-black controls; dark uses neutral greys (#121212, cards #1E1E1E) with light-grey controls. Color is reserved for each timer's own color and the orange of a finished timer.
  • PersistenceTimers are saved on the phone and come back after a reboot or app update.
  • StackKotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3. minSdk 33 (Android 13), targeting API 35.
